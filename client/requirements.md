## Notes
- Using 'Inter' for body text and 'Outfit' for headings to achieve a professional modern look.
- Sidebar navigation dynamically groups sites by Region (North, South, East, West).
- "Connectivity" is treated as a 1:1 relation (upsert logic).
- Other sub-resources (Services, Telephony, Diagrams) are 1:N relations.
